What is Scan-A-Scam?

A quick, reliable way to double-check suspicious messages before you click a link or enter your information. Whether you receive an unexpected giveaway notification, a job opportunity that seems off, or a weird security alert in your DMs, this tool scans the content and tells you immediately if it is safe or fraudulent.

  • DM & Text Scanning: Works for messages across Instagram, Discord, text messages, or email.
  • Red-Flag Detection: Identifies fake login portals, malicious links, and spoofed website domains.
  • Account Security: Protects your social media handles, gaming accounts, and personal data from being compromised.

How it Works

Verification takes under ten seconds and requires no account creation or login steps.

  1. Upload or Paste: Drop a screenshot of the suspicious message into the box, or paste the text directly.
  2. Automated Analysis: The system verifies the sender’s address, destination URLs, and common scam patterns.
  3. Direct Verdict: Receive a clear "Safe" or "Scam" rating alongside a brief summary of any detected risks.

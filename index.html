What is Scan-A-Scam?

A simple way to double-check suspicious messages before you click a link or share your information. Whether you receive an unexpected giveaway notification, a job opportunity that seems suspicious, or an unusual security alert, Scan-A-Scam helps you recognize common warning signs.

  • DM & Text Scanning: Check messages from social media, text messages, and email.
  • Red-Flag Detection: Look for suspicious links, urgent requests, and other common scam patterns.
  • Account Security: Learn how to protect your accounts and personal information.

How It Works

Checking a message is designed to be simple. No account or login is required.

  1. Upload or Paste: Upload a screenshot if supported by the scanner, or paste the suspicious message.
  2. Message Analysis: The demo checks for common scam warning signs.
  3. Understand the Risks: Review the warning signs and learn what to do next.

Note: The browser-only demo uses basic pattern matching. It cannot guarantee whether a message is safe or fraudulent.
